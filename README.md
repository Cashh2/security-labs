### About me

I'm a cybersecurity professional and **U.S. Army veteran** based in Indianapolis. My background covers SOC work, incident response, risk assessments against **NIST** and **CIS Controls**, and intelligence analysis. Right now I teach **Cybersecurity & Networking** to Early College High School students at MSD Wayne Township, where I also write the curriculum.

- 🛡️ **Blue team:** threat detection in a live SOC sandbox (OmniSOC), malware detonation, incident response playbooks
- 📋 **GRC:** CIS Level 1 assessments for local businesses; wrote Acceptable Use, Password, and Vendor Management policies aligned to NIST
- 🔓 **Offensive fundamentals:** SQL injection, stack buffer overflows, privilege and access-control misconfigurations, adversarial ML ([lab writeups ↓](#-featured-work))
- 🎓 **B.S. Cybersecurity & Global Policy**, Indiana University (Luddy School), 2025
- 🔭 **Building now:** network-security tooling in **Python, C, and JavaScript**
